Our mission

A community where every child can learn, thrive, and imagine a future full of possibility.

Who we are

Small acts of care can change a whole community.

Light for Community is a community-based initiative helping marginalized families access quality education and health services. We focus our energy where it is needed most: rebuilding schools and fighting malnutrition.

With compassion, practical support, and local partnership, we help children grow into their full potential.
