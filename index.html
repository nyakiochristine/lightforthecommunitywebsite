Our mission

Who we are

Created by young people who chose to act.

Light for the Community (LFC) is a youth-led NGO working with vulnerable and conflict-affected communities in the Democratic Republic of Congo. We were created because children should never have to choose between safety, health and learning.

Our story begins with the daily realities around us: damaged school infrastructure, families navigating health and nutrition challenges, and children whose opportunities are narrowed by conflict. We chose to turn concern into practical action, alongside the communities we serve.

Our vision: a future where every child has equitable access to quality education, health, dignity and opportunities.
